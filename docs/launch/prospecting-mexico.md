## Outreach positioning

Do not pitch Heimda11 as another agent builder.

Pitch:

> You already build the AI workers. Heimda11 gives you the workforce operations layer: identity, permissions, budgets, credentials, audit, performance and human approvals — self-hosted in each client's infrastructure.

## Short partner outreach

Subject: Control plane for the AI agents you deploy

Hi,

I saw that your team is already deploying AI agents and automation into real business operations.

I’m building Heimda11, a self-hosted Digital Workforce OS. It sits around existing agent stacks instead of replacing them, and gives each AI worker an identity, human owner, permissions, budget, scoped credentials, audit history and lifecycle.

The current release also includes human approval policies, model-cost tracking, operational events, encrypted secrets, provider fallback and governed multi-agent workflows.

I think the interesting fit for an implementation partner is that Heimda11 can become a reusable governance/operations layer across client deployments while the customer keeps the control plane and data inside its own infrastructure.

Community is available publicly; I’m opening a small Founding Company / design-partner group for teams already operating agents in production.

GitHub:
https://github.com/ricardoaldape/Heimda11

If this overlaps with what you are seeing in client projects, I’d be interested in comparing notes and testing one real deployment.

— Ricardo Aldape

## Qualification questions

For each conversation, learn:
1. How many agents are currently in production?
2. Who owns each agent operationally?
3. How are tool permissions / credentials handled?
4. How are model/API budgets assigned?
5. Which actions require human approval?
6. How do they attribute cost and value by agent?
7. How are agents retired and credentials revoked?
8. Would customer-controlled self-hosting make procurement easier?
9. Would they prefer Heimda11 as software, partner infrastructure, or both?

## Commercial target

First validation milestone: 5 paying Founding Companies at USD $500/month.

Avoid mass outreach. Personalize around the prospect's actual deployment model and one concrete pain Heimda11 can remove.
